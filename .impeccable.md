# Impeccable — Project Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Engineering teams using AI agents (Claude and other LLMs) as collaborative coders, plus the tech leads and architects who set their constraints. Visitors arrive at shapes.fyi to understand a specification — what Shapes is, how the graph works, and how to adopt it. They are technical, time-constrained, skim-then-deep readers. Secondary audience: research labs and editorial teams adopting the same spec for non-software domains.

The job to be done: *quickly grasp what Shapes is, why it exists, and how to start*, then dive into the full specification with confidence that the project is rigorous and well-built.

### Brand Personality
**Rigorous. Transparent. Structural.**

Voice: precise, declarative, agent-aware. Explains *why* before *what*. Honest about scope and non-goals. Scholarly without gatekeeping. Treats AI agents as first-class readers alongside humans.

Emotional goal: **quiet authority** — trust earned through restraint and craft, not through volume. The site should feel like a well-built specification, not a marketing funnel.

### Aesthetic Direction
Modern developer-tool flagship aesthetic in the lineage of **Linear, Vercel, Resend** — dense, confident, type-forward, crafted — but bent toward Shapes' literary/structural identity rather than pure tech minimalism. Serif-forward typography (Crimson Pro for reading, Cormorant Garamond for display) paired with a structural sans (Geist) for UI chrome and metadata creates a three-voice hierarchy: display serif / reading serif / UI sans.

Color: warm OKLCh foundation (earth + cream) with disciplined accent colors. Both light and dark modes as first-class citizens, with FOUC-free theme switching preserved.

**Anti-references — the site must NOT look like:**
- Generic SaaS landing pages (gradient hero, three feature cards, testimonial wall)
- Crypto / AI hype aesthetic (neon, glow, cyberpunk, holographic)
- Notion-clone docs (sidebar + slab content, undifferentiated)
- Brutalist Helvetica (all-caps, harsh grids, intentionally ugly)

### Design Principles
1. **Why before what.** Every section, component, and interaction earns its place by communicating intent. If it doesn't carry meaning, distill it out.
2. **Structure is the brand.** Grids, borders, hierarchies, and DAG-like relationships are visible — not decoration, but identity. The site should feel built, not arranged.
3. **Quiet authority over loud confidence.** Trust comes from restraint, type craft, and precision — not from gradients, glows, or motion for its own sake.
4. **Three voices, one system.** Display serif (Cormorant), reading serif (Crimson), and UI sans (Geist) each have a clear job. Never mix roles.
5. **Motion serves meaning.** Animations reveal structure, guide attention, or reward intent. Every motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and degrades to a still composition.
6. **Agents are first-class readers.** Copy, structure, and semantics must be legible to LLMs as well as humans — clear headings, honest scope, falsifiable claims.
7. **OKLCh discipline.** Every color flows from the token system. No arbitrary hex, no pure black, no pure white in borders or shadows.
